Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Lumblu app handles information. Lumblu is a creative app for children that includes drawing, coloring, puzzles, games, and related creative activities.

Information we collect

Lumblu stores your creations and game progress on your device. The Android app uses Google services for ads and optional purchases. The sections below explain what information those services may handle.

Accounts

Lumblu does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Photos, camera and gallery

Lumblu allows a user to choose an image from the device gallery or take a photo using the device's camera app for use in drawing and creative activities. These images are processed locally on the user's device. Lumblu does not upload these images to Lumblu servers.

Drawings and saved creations

Drawings, coloring work, game progress, and other app-created files are stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share them.

Sharing

Lumblu can open the device's standard sharing interface when the user chooses to share a creation. The user decides which external app or service receives the file. Once content is shared to another app or service, that third party's privacy practices apply.

Advertising

Lumblu uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. Lumblu requests child-directed ads with a general-audience content rating and disables access to the Android Advertising ID. These ad requests are configured to disable personalized advertising and remarketing.

Google may process information such as your IP address, which can indicate a general location, app and ad interactions, technical diagnostics, and other identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and help prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts the data it sends to Google in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes the optional Remove Ads purchase. Lumblu uses purchase information from Google Play, including the purchased item, purchase status, and a purchase reference, to confirm the purchase and restore it later. Google handles any payment details you enter in Google Play.

Children's privacy

Lumblu is designed for children, and the advertising settings described above apply to everyone using the app. Parents and guardians can contact us about their child’s information using the email address below.

Data retention and deletion

Drawings, saved creations, and game progress are kept on your device until you delete them or clear the app’s storage. Copies you export or share are managed separately. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and keep them as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information you provided to us. Google explains how it keeps and deletes information in its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If Lumblu changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@lumblu.com
